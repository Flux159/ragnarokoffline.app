The Abyss Chaser 4th job is here, plus quest fixes for Shadow Cross and Moscovia, and a roaming-companion fix.

## New

- **Abyss Chaser's 4th job change:** Shadow Chasers at Base 200 / Job 70 can now become Abyss Chasers through the official quest. It starts with Vicente in the Shadow Chaser guild and ends in the Abyssal Mansion instance. Renewal only. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#751)

## Fixes

- **Hunt quest messages:** killing one monster on a quest with several hunt targets no longer flashes another target's progress over your head (e.g. "Drainliar [Completed]" while hunting Zenorc). Thanks @AlwaysGH. (#233, #749)
- **Shadow Cross job quest:** the dialogue, cutins and overhead lines now follow the official quest. NPC sprites, positions and scene timing are corrected, the Shadow Manor entrance is moved, and the Job Quest markers show at Rumin and Verkhasel. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#752)
- **Moscovia quests and Abyss Chaser:** the Moscovia quests no longer share quest IDs with the new Abyss Chaser quest in Renewal, so neither blocks the other and the quest log shows the right titles. Moscovia quests already in progress carry over. Thanks @MondoTruth. (#753)
- **Roaming companions and Fly Wings:** a roaming companion that uses a Fly Wing now stays where it lands, instead of reappearing next to you with the wing spent. Thanks @AlwaysGH. (#755)
