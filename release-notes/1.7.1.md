## New
- **24th Anniversary in the registry:** kRO's 24th anniversary event. The Coin Exchanger in Prontera hands out 20 daily Attendance Boxes and a 24th Anniversary Gift Box, and trades the anniversary coins for potions, buffs, Tokens of Siegfried, two costume weapons and a Himmelmez wig in your choice of colour. A setting sends the boxes through the game's Attendance Check window instead, as on kRO. It installs from akreao's own repository. Renewal only. Thanks @akreao. (#696)

## Fixes
- **Catherina Von Blood and Bat Von Blood drain on ranged attacks too:** their HP and SP drain on a kill now also works with bows and guns, as on iRO, not only in melee and with magic. Renewal only. Thanks @akreao. (rathena#80 via #698)
