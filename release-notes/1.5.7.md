A worked example mod for changing stock skills, plus fixes for Doram weapons, mounts, map models, shadows, fog and item descriptions, and for warps and Fly Wings slowing down the longer you played.

## Modding
- **Changing stock skills, a worked example:** a new example mod, `skill-tuning`, shows how a mod changes existing skills without a server change: cast time, delay, duration, area and cost in its `db/` table, damage, accuracy and element through Lua hooks, different numbers for each era, and parts behind settings. Its README lists what is easy to get wrong and what a mod can't change yet. Thanks @BlaXun. (#540)

## Fixes
- **Doram weapons:** a Doram's weapon now shows. The client looked for it in the human sprite folder, where Doram weapons aren't kept. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#117 via #543)
- **Map models with repeated part names:** models whose parts share one name, such as some houses on Lasagna, are no longer drawn in pieces scattered away from the rest. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#118 via #543)
- **Shadows on bridges:** a character standing on a bridge or a deck is no longer darkened by the shadow the bridge casts on the ground below. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#119 via #543)
- **Garments on mounts:** a garment now changes with the mount, so a riding character shows the mounted version and goes back to the normal one when they dismount. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#120 via #543)
- **Item descriptions:** a description's map links (NAVI tags) are now links you can click, the way they are in NPC dialogue, instead of raw tags, and the item window no longer shows the last item's picture while the next one loads. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#121 via #544)
- **Coloured NPC links:** a link in NPC dialogue whose name is coloured no longer breaks the rest of the line or sends Navigation a garbled name. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#122 via #544)
- **Neutral cast aura:** the aura around a character casting a Neutral skill, such as Energy Coat, now spins like every other element's instead of sitting still. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#123 via #544)
- **Effects in fog:** on foggy maps such as Comodo's fields, glowing effects like Jupiter Thunder's ball no longer fly inside a pale square. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#124 via #544)
- **Warps and Fly Wings slowing down over time:** with an attendance event on (standart-npc turns one on), every map change redrew the hidden attendance window's 20 reward slots on top of the old ones, so each warp took longer the longer you played, until NPCs appeared well after the map. The window now starts from an empty list on each load. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#126 via #548, #545)
- **Weapons and shields on mounts:** a rider on an all-class mount (fox, lion, sheep and the rest) now holds their weapon, and anyone on any mount, a Peco or Gryphon included, shows their shield. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#127 via #548)
- **White Body effect:** the White Body enchant and the `EF_WHITEBODY` effect now make a character's body glow white instead of doing nothing. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#116 via #548)
- **Population clothes colours:** population players no longer show up in the default clothes colours because they were given a colour their class has no palette for. Thanks @akreao. (#547)
- **Leap onto a warp:** jumping onto a warp with a relocation skill such as Leap or Shadow Leap no longer leaves you standing at the old map's spot on the new map, often off its edge, until the next teleport. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#128 via #551)

## Known issues
- **Some iRO picture footprints and illustrations may still not show:** about 450 pictures stored uncompressed in iRO's `data.grf` (some NPC illustrations and the plain footprint effect among them) are still not served in this release. The fix is merged in the asset server but not released yet, so it ships in a later version.
