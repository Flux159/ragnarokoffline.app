The Shadow Cross 4th job quest, Alitea, Night Watch and Shinkiro gear that can finally be equipped, and a max stats setting that now covers every class.

## New
- **Shadow Cross job change quest:** Guillotine Crosses at Base 200 / Job 70 can now become Shadow Crosses through the official quest. It starts with Rumin in the Guillotine Cross guild and ends in the Shadow Manor instance. A field north of Veins that had no warps in Renewal can now be reached. Renewal only. Thanks @MondoTruth. (rathena#79 via #712)

## Modding
- **Friendly monsters:** `api.monsters.friendly(gid)` marks a monster your party owns, such as a pet a mod spawns for a companion. It gets the plain cursor instead of the attack one, and attack-nearest skips it. Thanks @AlwaysGH. (#700)
- **`equip` takes a position:** a script can say which hand or which accessory slot an item goes in, and with two of the same item it now puts on the one that isn't worn yet. Thanks @AlwaysGH. (rathena#82 via #712)

## Fixes
- **Max stats covers every class:** raising the max stats setting now raises the cap for rebirth classes, trans and baby third jobs, and fourth jobs too. Before, they kept their usual caps however high it went. (#710)
- **Alitea, Night Watch and Shinkiro gear can be equipped:** 53 regional items for these jobs were marked as fourth-job gear, so no one could wear them. The Paradise Mace can now be used by the Druid line too. Thanks @LilArt420. (rathena#77 via #712)
- **Kachua's Secret Box opens:** the iRO box now gives the same rewards as rAthena's own Kachua's Secret Box instead of saying its contents aren't available. Thanks @LilArt420. (rathena#83 via #712)
- **Pets and summons aren't enemies:** companions and the town population no longer treat a monster a player owns (an Alchemist's sphere, a mod's pet) as a fight to join or a reason to cast. Thanks @AlwaysGH. (#699)
- **Shells a mod controls survive a warp:** a population character a mod's script is controlling no longer vanishes when the script warps it. Thanks @3122380113. (#694, fixes #693)
