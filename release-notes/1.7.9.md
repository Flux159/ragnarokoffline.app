Mods can now swap out stock scripts, and the Imperial Guard and Dragon Knight job quests follow the official quests, with the firefly effect now drawn.

## Modding

- **Mods can switch stock scripts off:** list stock NPC or monster-spawn files in a new `stock-npc-remove.txt` and ship your own copy instead, e.g. to change a map's spawn table. The navigation tables follow the change. See *Switching stock scripts on and off* in the modding guide. (#758, with Flux159/rathena#91: rAthena's `delnpc` in the map server config ran before the stock scripts loaded and removed nothing)

## Fixes

- **Imperial Guard job quest:** the dialogue now follows the official quest, the Job Quest markers show on the King's Knight and both knights, and each opponent appears in the arena before their round. You now have to give both knights the same answer; contradictory answers send you back to talk to them again. Thanks @MondoTruth. (Flux159/rathena#89 via #757)
- **Dragon Knight job quest:** the dialogue, announcements and quest titles now follow the official quest. Egg communion drains HP and SP faster, as it does officially, and Leticia takes reduced damage for the first 15 seconds of the final fight. After a failed attempt you can talk to her again to retry. The finale shows the real firefly effect instead of a stand-in. Thanks @MondoTruth. (Flux159/rathena#92 via #759)
- **Firefly effect:** `EF_FIREFLY` (effect 45) now draws faint drifting particles around the target. Before, the client drew nothing for it. Thanks @MondoTruth. (Flux159/roBrowserLegacy#234 via #760)
