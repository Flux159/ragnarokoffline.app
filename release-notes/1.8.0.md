Companions and the other people in the world can now be told to keep quiet.

## New

- **Quieter companions:** Settings → Population has two new switches, **Companion chat** for the companions in your party and **Population chat** for everyone else. Both are on by default. Turned off, they stop the chatter, the replies when you say a name or whisper, the kill taunts and the tactics lines. Companions still answer what you tell them to do, and a roaming companion still tells you when it's down. (#764)

## Fixes

- **Graphics+ looking blurry:** Graphics+ has a new **Screen effects** switch for everything it draws over the finished picture (grading, glow, haze, tone mapping, vignette and the lens effects). Turned off, the game draws straight to the screen and grass, water, shadows and trees still work. The blur most likely comes from Graphics+ together with the game's own Performance mode, which draws that picture at three-quarter size; turning either one off should fix it. (#763)
