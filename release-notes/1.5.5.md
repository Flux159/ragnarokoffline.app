Favorite mods, a server clock that follows your time zone, an optional bag of its own for each companion, and a market fed by simulated hunting parties, plus fixes for the chat after F10, the skill hotbar, boxes with a choice of rewards, NPC menus, costumes after a restart, and the attendance window.

## New
- **Favorite mods:** star a mod in Settings → Mods (Installed or Find Mods) and find it again in the new Favorites tab, installed or not, with a button to jump to it. Stars are only a list for you: they don't switch a mod on. (#509)
- **Server clock:** Settings → General → Server clock sets the time zone the game server keeps, for War of Emperium, timed events, daily resets and mods that follow the time of day. It follows your computer by default; before, the server always ran on UTC. **One-time shift:** on the first start after updating, a server that ran on UTC switches to your time zone, so one day's reset (attendance, dailies) may come a few hours early or late, once. Thanks @BlaXun. (#495)
- **Companion inventory (optional):** Settings → Population → Companion inventory, off by default, gives each recruited companion a bag of its own: no free potion or ammunition refills, skills cost their catalysts and arrows, it keeps what you trade it, and its bag is saved with it. Items traded to a companion can't be taken back yet. Thanks @BlaXun. (#484)

## Modding
- **Up to 40 settings per mod:** a mod's `mod.json` may now declare 40 settings, up from 20. Thanks @BlaXun. (#511, fixes #492)
- **More for companion strategies (experimental):** `Sit`, `Claim`, rules that look at how long a strategy or fight has run, renewing a buff before it lapses, and rules that respect the skills you've ticked for a companion. Thanks @BlaXun. (#483)
- **Attendance that repeats:** a new rAthena extension, `attendance_repeat` (off by default), starts the attendance rewards again at day 1 after the last day, so one long period gives its rewards in a cycle. Thanks @MondoTruth. (rathena#29 via #497)
- **prontera-vendors 1.5.0:** an optional "hunted supply" fills the market with monster loot brought in by simulated hunting parties, and mods can now cap how much of an item their stalls list (`$@pop_item_supply`). Thanks @BlaXun. (#491)

## Fixes
- **Chat after F10:** hiding the chat with F10 and bringing it back no longer moves it off the top of the screen. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#96 via #497, fixes #488)
- **Hotbar next to the chat:** the skill hotbar now snaps against the chat window's visible frame instead of overlapping it. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#98 via #497, fixes #490)
- **Boxes with a choice of rewards:** selectable item packages now open a window to pick the reward set, instead of doing nothing, when the client's data has the package table. Thanks @Dizzzlectic. (roBrowserLegacy#99 via #502, for #500)
- **NPC menus:** clicking the empty space below an NPC menu's choices and then OK (or double-clicking it) sent an invalid choice, which could get you disconnected. Clicks outside the choices are now ignored. Thanks @akreao. (roBrowserLegacy#100 via #507)
- **Costumes after a restart:** after a full restart of the game, newer costume headgear and garments could be missing on your own character until you went back to character select. They now show from the start. Thanks @MondoTruth. (roBrowserLegacy#101 via #512)
- **Attendance that never ends:** an attendance period longer than a year (such as a server that always offers attendance) now shows "Ongoing" and ∞ in the attendance window, instead of an end date years away and thousands of days left. Thanks @MondoTruth. (roBrowserLegacy#102 via #513)
- **Companions sitting by a strategy rule** stay seated instead of bobbing up and down. (#483)
