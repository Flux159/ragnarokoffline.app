Your Discord profile can now show what you're playing, friends can ask to join your world from Discord, and your world can post its big moments to a Discord channel.

## New
- **Discord Rich Presence:** while you play in the app, your Discord profile shows your job, levels, map and party. Turn it off, or choose what it shows, in Settings → General → Discord. Your character's name stays hidden unless you turn it on. Friends who join through the app get their own status too. Thanks @akreao. (#733)
- **Ask to Join on Discord:** with Share with friends running and **Ask to Join** turned on (it's off by default), friends see an "Ask to Join" button on your Discord status. You choose whether to let them in, and their app asks them before joining. Thanks @akreao. (#735)
- **Discord announcements:** give Settings → General → Discord announcements a channel webhook, and your world posts to that channel when it goes online or offline. The new **discord-announcements** mod in Settings → Mods → Find Mods adds logins, level milestones, MVP kills and War of Emperium. Thanks @akreao. (#734)

## Modding
- **Announce from any mod:** a script line `debugmes "[discord] ..."` is posted to the announcements channel when one is set. Thanks @akreao. (#734)
