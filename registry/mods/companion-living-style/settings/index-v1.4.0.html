Companion Living Style v1.4.0 — test build

Keep Companion Party Overhaul enabled. Save here, then use Apply & restart in the app.

Choose upper, middle, lower and garment looks separately below. Costumes you equip on them take priority.

Shuffle range: 1–9999. The number chooses outfits, not how quickly they appear. Map arrival requests an immediate refresh; newly spawned AI are checked in small batches about every 250 ms. Actual rendering/network timing can vary.

Town themes: ambient townsfolk in major towns mix in costumes that suit the town, about 60% per slot. A Costume Guide stands beside each town's Warpra or Kafra and can switch that town's theme off or on, with no GM commands needed.

Town life: AI characters sit for a short rest when no monster is near, and townsfolk near you show the odd emote. Both are light on the server: sitting uses the engine's own rules, and emotes only look around you.
