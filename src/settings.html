Which rules the server runs

Each era keeps its own characters. Switching does not convert them — your other era's characters stay where they are and come back when you switch back. They cannot be shared: pre-renewal stops at level 99 and has no third or fourth jobs. Switching restarts the server.

Your link —

Send that link to your friends. They can paste it into this app's Join a friend box, or open it in a browser and play there without installing anything.

Off by default: the server listens only on this machine. Turning this on makes it reachable by anyone who can reach your computer on the network. The engine reads this setting when it starts, so use Restart server to apply a change.

Server address

Paste whatever your friend sent — the whole link or just the address. Joining runs nothing locally — no server, no virtual machine, no game files. Everything comes from the host, so this starts in seconds. Register on their login screen by adding _M or _F to a new username.

Mods

Looking…

A mod is a folder. Drop one in and it appears here. Server changes need the server restarted, which Apply does; client changes — sprites, styling — need the app restarted.

Population

They walk, fight, sit and open vending stalls, and they never touch your characters or your save. They appear only on the maps you and your friends are actually standing on. How busy scales the server's own spawn tables, so the world keeps the shape it was authored with and only its crowding changes; Limit is a safety ceiling across every map at once, and in a solo game it never comes into play.

Rates

Both raise only. Attack speed is 190 by default and 199 is the server's ceiling — the difference is small but it is the whole gap between fast and instant. Stat cap is 99 by default and goes to 32767; third, baby and summoner classes are lifted with it, and left at their own defaults while this is untouched.

Rates are written to rAthena's conf/import/battle_conf.txt, which overrides the shipped config. Applying restarts the map server, so log back in afterwards.
