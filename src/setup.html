Play on someone else's server. You need nothing but the link they give you — no game files, no download. Their machine serves the client and the artwork, so this starts in seconds.

Server address from your friend, e.g. http://192.168.1.20:3338/

You will make your own character on their server. On the login screen, add _M to the end of a new username to register as male or _F for female — the account is created as you log in.

English text is built in; it does not come from these files.
