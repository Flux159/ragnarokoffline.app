# Settings: Rates

How fast you level, what monsters drop, a couple of caps, and how far you can see.

Rates are written to rAthena's `conf/import/battle_conf.txt`, which overrides
the shipped config. Applying restarts the map server, so log back in afterwards.

## Experience

**Base**, **Job** and **Quest**, as multipliers. 1x is the stock game.

Any EXP rate above 1x also lifts the server's one-level-per-kill cap. Without
that, a kill grants one level and **discards** everything the monster gave
beyond it — so at 50x you would gain a single level per monster and lose the
rest, which reads as the slider not working.

## Drops

**Item**, **Equipment** and **Card**, as multipliers. Boss, heal, use, MVP and
treasure rates follow the common rate rather than getting sliders of their own.

## Monsters drop Zeny

rAthena has no Zeny multiplier: whether monsters drop it at all is a switch, and
the amount derives from the monster's level.

## Attack speed cap

190 by default, 199 is the server's ceiling. The difference is small and is the
whole gap between fast and instant.

**Raise only.** Third, baby and summoner classes have their own stock ceilings
and are lifted with it, rather than being dragged down to yours.

## Stat cap

99 by default, up to 32767. Third, baby, extended and summoner classes are
lifted with it for the same reason — setting only the base cap would quietly
nerf third jobs on an untouched install.

## View distance

How far away the server shows you monsters, players and NPCs, for screens wider
than 16:9. A wider window already draws more of the map, but the server only
sends what is within 14 cells, so on a 21:9 or 32:9 screen the edges are empty
ground you cannot click.

| Preset | Shown | Click-to-walk | Monster sight and chase |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Official** (default) | 14 cells | 17 cells | 1x |
| **Wide** (21:9) | 20 cells | 20 cells | 1.4x |
| **Ultrawide** (32:9) | 28 cells | 24 cells | 2x |

One preset rather than four numbers because they only work together. A view
past monster sight means hitting monsters that never react; a view past the walk
limit means clicking ground you can see and not moving. These are rAthena's
`area_size`, `max_walk_path`, `view_range_rate` and `chase_range_rate`.

- Click-to-walk stops at 24 even on Ultrawide. The server cannot go past 32, and
  close to that it and the client stop agreeing on paths around obstacles.
- Separately, the server always stops a walk at 14 cells when something blocks the
  straight line. That is compiled into rAthena and no setting reaches it.
- The client draws skill effects only within 20 cells of you, so on Ultrawide
  a monster at the edge of the screen casts without the animation.
- Wider views mean more monsters awake at once. Official is the stock game.
- It applies to your own server. On a friend's server you get their setting.
