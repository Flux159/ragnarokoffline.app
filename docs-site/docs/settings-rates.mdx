# Settings: Rates

How fast you level, what monsters drop, and a couple of caps.

Rates are written to rAthena's `conf/import/battle_conf.txt`, which overrides
the shipped config. Applying restarts the map server, so log back in afterwards.

## Experience

**Base**, **Job** and **Quest**, as multipliers. 1x is the stock game.

Any EXP rate above 1x also lifts the server's one-level-per-kill cap. Without
that, a kill grants one level and **discards** everything the monster gave
beyond it — so at 50x you would gain a single level per monster and lose the
rest, which reads as the slider not working.

## Drops

**Item**, **Equipment** and **Card**, as multipliers. Boss, heal, use, MVP and
treasure rates follow the common rate rather than getting sliders of their own.

## Monsters drop Zeny

rAthena has no Zeny multiplier: whether monsters drop it at all is a switch, and
the amount derives from the monster's level.

## Attack speed cap

190 by default, 199 is the server's ceiling. The difference is small and is the
whole gap between fast and instant.

**Raise only.** Third, baby and summoner classes have their own stock ceilings
and are lifted with it, rather than being dragged down to yours.

## Stat cap

99 by default, up to 32767. Third, baby, extended and summoner classes are
lifted with it for the same reason — setting only the base cap would quietly
nerf third jobs on an untouched install.

## Free Kafra services

Warps and storage at no cost. The prices are hardcoded in the NPC script with no
config behind them, so the app rewrites the script from a pristine copy on every
start.
